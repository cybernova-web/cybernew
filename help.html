CYBERSCHOOL SUPPORT

Something Went Wrong Online?

Don't panic. You are not alone. Choose the situation that is closest to your problem and learn what you can do.

🛡️

Remember: Ask for Help

If something online makes you feel unsafe, uncomfortable or threatened, tell a trusted adult, teacher, parent or guardian.

COMMON ONLINE PROBLEMS

What Do You Need Help With?

💬

Cyberbullying

Someone is insulting, threatening or harassing you online.

  1. Stay calm and do not reply with insults.
  2. Save screenshots or other evidence.
  3. Block and report the person.
  4. Tell a trusted adult or teacher.
🎣

Suspicious Link or Message

You received a strange link, email or message.

  1. Do not click the link.
  2. Do not enter personal information.
  3. Delete or report the message.
  4. Ask a trusted adult if you are unsure.
🔑

Account Problem

Someone may have accessed your account or you cannot access it.

  1. Change your password if possible.
  2. Sign out of unknown devices.
  3. Enable two-factor authentication.
  4. Tell a trusted adult or teacher.
👤

Personal Information Shared

You accidentally shared private information online.

  1. Do not share more information.
  2. Delete the post or message if possible.
  3. Change passwords if necessary.
  4. Tell a trusted adult.
🦠

Possible Malware

Your device behaves strangely after downloading something.

  1. Stop using the suspicious file.
  2. Do not install unknown software.
  3. Tell a trusted adult.
  4. Use appropriate security software.
🚨

Stranger Contact

Someone you do not know is asking for personal information.

  1. Do not share personal information.
  2. Stop communicating with them.
  3. Block and report the account.
  4. Tell a trusted adult immediately.
❤️

YOU DON'T HAVE TO HANDLE IT ALONE

Talk to Someone You Trust

A parent, guardian, teacher or another trusted adult can help you decide what to do next.
